The program

BUILT IN THE BOROUGHS.

Across New York City, Bandits players train in quality facilities, face meaningful competition and take the field prepared to play with confidence.

Across the city

One city. Five home fields.

The Bandits play throughout the Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens and Staten Island.

Every summer

COOPERSTOWN.

Every summer our 12U Bandits head to Cooperstown for a national, weeklong tournament in the Hall of Fame’s backyard. It is baseball, travel and team life packed into one unforgettable week.

2023 season

A CHAMPIONSHIP SUMMER.

Bandits teams celebrated Summer League championships in 2023—a season built on committed coaching, player development and teammates showing up for one another.

Brooklyn Little League

OPENING DAY, TOGETHER.

EEP Law sponsored three Youth Sports NYC teams—8U, 10U and 12U. Opening day brought players and families into the street for a parade and onto the field for a new season.
